# **Episode 3:**

## *Hunger*

Morning brought no comfort.

Only clarity.

The tracks circling the lake looked even larger in daylight. Four toes. Deep claws. Heavy enough to sink into damp earth without hesitation.

He crouched beside one of the prints.

> *...Too big.*

He had no name for the creature.

Only the certainty that he never wanted to meet it up close.

His stomach answered with a sharp growl.

Food.

Water.

Shelter.

The order suddenly seemed obvious.

He knelt beside the lake and cupped water into his hands.

Cold.

Clear.

He hesitated.

Could he trust it?

A memory flickered.

Not a face.

Not a place.

Just a pair of dirty hands pouring water into a pot over a fire.

> *Boil it...*

The thought vanished as quickly as it had appeared.

He stared at the lake.

His options were limited.

He drank anyway.

The water was icy enough to sting his teeth but left no strange taste behind.

Good enough.

For now.



----



The forest looked different beneath the morning sun.

Birds called from the canopy.

Bright insects drifted between flowers.

Small animals darted through bushes before disappearing again.

Life was everywhere.

Finding something edible was another matter entirely.

He picked up a bright red berry.

It looked harmless.

Another memory surfaced.

> 
> *A child crying.*
> 
> *An adult's voice shouting.*
> 
> *Red... white spots... don't...*
> 

Gone.

He dropped the berry immediately.

>"I'll assume that's bad."

Guessing wrong only needed to happen once.

Instead, he searched for signs rather than food itself.

Broken branches.

Animal trails.

Fresh droppings.

Places where life gathered.

Hours passed.

Eventually he found a narrow stream feeding the lake.

Unlike the shoreline, its muddy banks were covered in tracks.

Small hoofprints.

Tiny pawprints.

Bird tracks.

Everything came here eventually.

If animals trusted this place...

Perhaps he could too.

As he followed the stream upstream, sunlight caught something impossible.

Stone.

Not natural stone.

Cut stone.

Hidden beneath vines stood the remains of a narrow bridge crossing the stream.

Its center had long since collapsed, but the remaining pillars still bore faint carvings.

Circles.

Stars.

Lines that intersected with impossible precision.

He brushed away centuries of moss.

The instant his fingers touched the carving...

Pain exploded behind his eyes.

> 
> *A city.*
> 
> *Towering walls.*
> 
> *Crowds.*
> 
> *Banners dancing in the wind.*
> 
> *A bell ringing.*
> 
> *Fire.*
> 
> *Screaming.*
> 

The vision shattered.

He staggered backward, breathing hard.

The bridge stood silent once more.

Nothing moved.

Nothing glowed.

Yet he knew what he had seen wasn't imagination.

This place had belonged to someone.

Someone long gone.

He looked beyond the ruined bridge.

The forest continued undisturbed.

But now...

He wasn't walking through untouched wilderness.

He was walking through the bones of a forgotten civilization.

Far above, hidden among the branches, a pair of golden eyes watched without blinking.

He never noticed.

Not once.

The forest had noticed him first.

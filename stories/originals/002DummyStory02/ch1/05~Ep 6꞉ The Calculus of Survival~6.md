# **Episode 6:**

## *The Calculus of Survival*

The rain hammered against the warped wood.

Relentless.

Almost deliberate in how it sealed him inside.

He sat in the dim stillness.

The metallic taste of the forced meal still clung to his mouth.

His stomach no longer ached.

His mind did.

Why must this happen?

How long does the body last without it?

The sequence made no sense.

Yet it remained.

If the machine stopped taking fuel, did it simply... end?

Shadows lengthened.

The storm choked the remaining daylight.

Darkness was a rising tide.

What moves out there when the light dies?

Can walls truly stop them?

Without light, the night belonged to things with heavy claws and silent footfalls.

A shelter felt like a box.

Or perhaps a trap.

He looked toward the remnants of the carcass tucked in the corner.

How many days will this buy?

What happens when the meat is gone?

A stockpile only delayed an unknown end.

Hours bled away in silence.

Slowly, the heavy drumming of the rain thinned.

Fading into a steady drip.

Before stopping altogether.

When the gray light of the clearing storm filtered through the broken entrance, he stood up.

Where do they go when the rain stops?

Can a hunt be planned, or is it only luck?

The next step remained entirely unclear.

Still, he had to move.

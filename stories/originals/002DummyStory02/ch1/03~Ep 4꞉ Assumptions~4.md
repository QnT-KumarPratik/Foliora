# ***Episode 4:*** 

## Assumptions

Morning stretched across the abandoned city in muted gold.

He crossed the broken bridge and stepped onto a road unlike anything he had seen since awakening.

The stones fit together too perfectly.

Even after untold years, they remained almost level beneath his feet.

> *"...Impossible."*

Buildings surrounded him.

Collapsed.

Broken.

Yet... not destroyed.

Their walls still stood where ordinary structures should have become little more than piles of rubble.

He placed a hand against cold stone.

His fingers traced grooves carved with impossible precision.

Not by nature.

Not by chance.

His chest tightened.

A strange certainty settled over him.

This shouldn't exist.

Not because it was magical.

Because it was... too well made.

A thought surfaced from somewhere buried deep within him.

> *"People couldn't build like this..."*

He frowned.

> *"People?*
> *Which people?"*

He searched his memory.

Nothing.

The thought vanished, leaving only unease.

He wandered deeper through the silent streets.

Everywhere he looked, the city contradicted itself.

The roads had survived.

The roofs had not.

Glass lay scattered in glittering fragments, yet the supporting walls remained standing.

Nature had reclaimed the courtyards, but vines avoided certain stones as though refusing to touch them.

He couldn't understand it.

If this place was truly ancient...

Why hadn't time finished its work?

He stopped in the center of the avenue.

> *"...Maybe it isn't that old."*

It was the first real conclusion he had reached since waking.

Or perhaps...

It was simply built to outlive those who made it.

Without realizing it, he opened the small notebook he'd found among the supplies.

On the first empty page, he wrote.

### Assumptions

- *Today is Day 2.*
- *The weather suggests early spring.*
- *This city belonged to a civilization more advanced than I expected.*
- *The ruins have not degraded as much as they should have.*
- *Either this place is younger than it appears...*
- *...or something has preserved it.*


He stared at the final sentence.

Then added one more.

- *None of this is certain.*

Closing the notebook, he looked toward the city's heart.

If he wanted answers...

He would have to stop assuming.

And start proving.

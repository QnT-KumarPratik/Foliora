# **Episode 2:***

## *The First Night*

The forest had grown quiet.

Not peaceful.

Quiet in the way a predator becomes quiet before it moves.

He stood where the cave overlooked the lake, the last orange light fading behind unfamiliar mountains. Shadows stretched across the ground, swallowing details one by one.

Three choices.

The cave.

The lake.

The trees.

He looked at each in silence.

The cave had sheltered him once, but it had also hidden skeletons and a pit deep enough to break bones. If something lived there now, darkness would be its ally.

The lake offered clean water, but nothing else. An open shoreline was an invitation for anything hunting after sunset.

That left the trees.

Using fallen branches as support, he climbed the nearest sturdy tree. The bark tore at his palms, and more than once he nearly slipped back to the ground. Eventually he found a pair of thick branches that crossed naturally.

It would have to do.

He tied himself loosely to the trunk using a length of old rope from the backpack. If he rolled over while asleep, at least he wouldn't fall.

Below him, the forest slowly disappeared beneath darkness.

The sounds came first.

Leaves rustling without wind.

Branches snapping somewhere in the distance.

A long, low cry echoed through the valley.

Nothing about it sounded familiar.

His hand instinctively tightened around the spear.

Sleep refused to come.

Hours crawled by.

The moon climbed overhead, bathing the forest in pale silver light.

Movement.

Near the lake.

He leaned forward carefully.

Several figures emerged from the trees.

Four-legged.

Too large to be wolves.

They lowered their heads to drink from the lake, every movement cautious, deliberate.

One suddenly stopped.

Its ears twitched.

Its head slowly turned toward the tree.

His breathing stopped.

The creature stared into the darkness.

Seconds passed.

Then it returned to drinking.

Eventually the small pack disappeared back into the forest as silently as they had arrived.

Only after they were gone did he realize his hands were shaking.

This world had predators.

Real ones.

And if creatures like those wandered near the lake every night...

Choosing the trees had probably saved his life.

Exhaustion finally overcame fear.

His eyes closed.

...

A voice whispered.

"...Find me..."

His eyes snapped open.

Morning sunlight filtered through the leaves.

The forest looked harmless again.

As though nothing had happened.

But near the edge of the lake...

Fresh tracks remained pressed into the mud.

Large.

Deep.

Undeniably real.

Whatever had watched him during the night...

It was still somewhere out there.

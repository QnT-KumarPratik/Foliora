# **Episode 5:**

## *Sustenance*

A low rumble echoed through the city.

He froze.

His head snapped toward the nearest street.

Nothing.

The buildings remained silent.

Another rumble.

He looked around again.

Nothing.

Then it came once more, this time from much closer.

He looked down.

His stomach growled.

He stared at it.

"...Oh."

He lowered his spear.

He was hungry.

He left the city.

The forest was quieter than before.

He kept his spear ready as he searched for something small enough to hunt and carry.

It took some time.

Then he saw movement.

A creature stood between the trees.

Four-legged.

Lean.

Fast.

Brown fur covered with pale spots.

He didn't know what it was called.

It noticed him.

For a moment, neither moved.

Then it ran.

He chased it.

Branches struck his face. Roots caught his feet. The creature was fast, far faster than he expected, but eventually his persistence won.

He threw the spear.

The creature stumbled.

He closed the distance and finished it.

Silence returned.

He stood over the body, breathing heavily.

His hands trembled.

He had hunted it.

A thought followed.

I hunted it.

His eyes moved through the trees.

*I can be hunted too...*

He looked around.

The forest suddenly felt much larger.

And much less safe.

He grabbed the animal and began dragging it toward the city.

Rain began to fall before he reached it.

At first, only a few drops.

Then more.

He hurried.

By the time he reached the city, the rain had become heavy enough to obscure the streets.

He needed somewhere dry.

He approached one of the houses.

The entrance was blocked.

He stared at it.

He didn't know how it worked.

He looked around.

Stone.

Hard.

Wood.

He walked around the building, examining the walls.

The bricks wouldn't give.

The wood might.

He raised his spear.

Hit it.

The wood shook.

He struck again.

Harder.

Again.

A crack appeared.

He stared at it.

Another strike.

The frame splintered.

One more.

The wood finally broke apart.

He dragged the animal inside and pulled what remained of the broken entrance behind him.

The rain continued outside.

He stood still for a moment.

Dry.

Safe enough... or not...

His eyes adjusted to the darkness.

The inside of the house was strange.

Objects filled the rooms.

Tables.

Shelves.

Containers.

Things he couldn't name.

He picked one up.

Turned it over.

Nothing.

Another object.

Nothing.

He moved deeper into the house.

A room contained more unfamiliar things.

He reached toward one.

Something sharp caught his skin.

He jerked his hand away.

A thin line of blood appeared.

He stared at it.

Sharp.

That much he understood.

He looked toward the animal.

Then back at the object.

Slowly, cautiously, he picked it up again.

He brought it to the carcass.

The edge cut through the flesh.

His stomach tightened at the sight.

But he continued.

Piece by piece.

When he finally had something he could eat, he stared at it.

There was no fire.

No preparation.

Nothing else he understood.

He ate.

He immediately regretted it.

The taste was awful.

His face twisted as he forced himself to swallow.

He nearly spat it out.

But he didn't.

He ate another piece.

Then another.

It tasted terrible.

It didn't matter.

He needed food.

He needed to live.

So he kept eating.

Outside, rain hammered against the ruined city.

Inside, the stranger sat alone in an abandoned house, eating something he had killed with a weapon he barely understood.

He knew almost nothing about this world.

But he was beginning to understand one thing.

Survival came before answers.
